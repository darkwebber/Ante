# Code of Conduct

## Mana promise

"Ante" community lo andaru welcome. Age, gender, caste, religion, region, language, experience level, appearance, sexual orientation, background, emi ayina sare. Evaru vachina, harassment lekunda, respect tho treat cheyyali. Idhi mana andari promise.

"Ante" Telugu meedha build ayina language, kaani ikkada **andaru welcome**. Telugu raani vaallu, vere dialect maatlade vaallu, ippude coding nerchukuntunna vaallu, andaru.

## Ilaa undali

- Vere vaalla opinions, experiences respect cheyyandi.
- Feedback ivvandi, theeskondi. Rendu kuda polite ga.
- Mistake chesthe accept cheyyandi, sorry cheppandi, nerchukondi.
- Mee okkari kosam kaadhu, community motham kosam edhi better o adhi aalochinchandi.
- Dialect gurinchi fights vaddhu. Vocabulary discussion lo "maa ooru lo ilaa antaaru" ani cheppandi, "nuvvu wrong" ani kaadhu.

## Ivi allowed kaadhu

- Sexual language, unwanted attention, or advances.
- Trolling, insults, personal or political attacks.
- Public ga or private ga harass cheyyadam.
- Evari personal info (address, phone, email) vaalla permission lekunda share cheyyadam.
- Caste, religion, region, or language meedha discrimination.
- Professional space lo correct kaani inka edhaina behavior.

## Maintainers em chesthaaru

Ee rules ni maintainers enforce chesthaaru. Ee Code of Conduct ki against ga unna comments, commits, code, issues, PRs ni edit, delete, or reject chese right vaallaki undhi. Enduku chesaro reason kuda chepthaaru.

## Ekkada apply avthundhi

Project lo prathi chota (GitHub issues, PRs, discussions, chat) apply avthundhi. Evaraina bayata "Ante" ni represent chesthunnappudu (official account, events) kuda apply avthundhi.

## Report cheyyadam

Edhaina problem ayina behavior choosthe, maintainers ki **[siddhutime1@gmail.com](mailto:siddhutime1@gmail.com)** ki report cheyyandi. Prathi complaint ni review chesi, fair ga, twaraga reply isthaam. Report chesina vaalla privacy ni safe ga unchuthaam.

## Em action theesukuntaam

| Stage | Em jarigindhi | Em avthundhi |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Correction | Chinna mistake, unprofessional language | Private warning, enduku wrong o explain chesthaam. Public sorry adagochu. |
| 2. Warning | Oka serious violation, or chinna violations malli malli | Warning. Konni rojulu aa person tho interaction undadhu. Malli jarigithe temporary ban. |
| 3. Temporary ban | Serious violation, or bad behavior continue avvadam | Konni rojulu community nunchi ban. |
| 4. Permanent ban | Harassment, repeated violations, groups meedha discrimination | Permanent ban. |
